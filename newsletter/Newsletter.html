Study: Evaluating a Dedicated Oncofertility AfterCare Clinic for Childhood Cancer Survivors

This study will examine the impact of attending a dedicated survivorship fertility clinic.

  • Primary objective: Assess changes in fertility-related concerns
  • Secondary objectives: Examine FP procedure uptake, barriers to FP, and interest in psychosocial and peer-support resources

Methods and participants: Using a pre–post survey design, chart review, and semi-structured interviews, the study will compare participants' reproductive concern scores before and after attending a specialist fertility clinic. Participants will include adolescents and young adults with a history of childhood cancer seen in the POGO AfterCare clinic at SickKids or Princess Margaret Cancer Centre.

Questions? Contact Maggie McNeill at Maggie.mcneill@sickkids.ca

Fertility Preservation Learning Tool for At-Risk Males

We are excited to share that our needs assessment from healthcare providers, patients, and caregivers has been completed. Our research team is developing a learning tool to support education on fertility preservation for at-risk males, which we plan to pilot and evaluate in 2027.
